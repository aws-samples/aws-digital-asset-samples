Key management & custody

Use case

Secure private-key custody and transaction signing for blockchain wallets.

How it works

  1. 1 Generate or import private keys in a hardened environment using AWS KMS or Nitro Enclaves.
  2. 2 Sign blockchain transactions without exposing key material.
  3. 3 Restrict signing authority through KMS key policies or enclave attestation.

Delivery versus Payment (DvP)

Use case

Settle tokenized securities atomically, so the asset and payment transfer together or not at all.

How it works

  1. 1 Escrow both legs of a trade—tokens and stablecoin—using a smart contract.
  2. 2 Validate the KYC allowlist, manage timeouts, and log audit events.
  3. 3 Execute an atomic swap or refund in a single transaction.

Agentic serverless payments

Use case

Enable conversational AI agents to purchase goods, settle USDC micropayments, and process refunds autonomously.

How it works

  1. 1 Authenticate the user and provision an embedded wallet with spending limits.
  2. 2 Let the agent browse products, handle HTTP 402 responses, and initiate payment through the x402 protocol.
  3. 3 Sign and settle USDC on-chain through the x402 facilitator.

Derived equities backed by verifiable reserves

Use case

Issue a tokenized equity backed by verifiable price and reserve data.

How it works

  1. 1 Expose equity-price and reserve data through AWS services.
  2. 2 Use Chainlink CRE to deliver feeds and verify collateral on-chain.
  3. 3 Mint, redeem, and rebalance synthetic stock tokens against the verified reserves.
